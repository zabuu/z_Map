# Artwork notice

Gathering resource identities are generated from the installed client's gameobject cache and Lock.dbc, with uncached identity/skill metadata from Shagu's MIT-licensed pfQuest and the installed pfQuest-turtle database. No spawn coordinates are copied. The original pfQuest MIT notice is included in `ThirdParty-pfQuest.txt`. `Media/GatherIcons31` contains circular full-color and grayscale copies of four native client inventory icons, with transparent corners. Their source artwork remains the property of its respective owners.

The world map paintings, exploration overlays, minimap terrain/building renders and interface icons originate from the user's installed World of Warcraft / OctoWoW client. They remain the property of their respective owners and are not covered by zMap's source-code license.

`Media/Atlas11` contains the native world overview and downsampled terrain mosaics. `Media/Atlas13` contains locally blended opaque painting pyramids over a procedurally generated ocean background. `Media/GroupCircle.tga` is a code-generated geometric marker. Full resolution minimap textures and isolated map art load directly from the client archives. No AI-generated map artwork is used.

`Media/Interiors20` contains locally assembled copies of the client's underground map paintings and exploration overlays, with smooth edge transparency. Their artwork remains the property of its respective owners.

`Media/Caves21` contains locally assembled and georeferenced WMO cave/mining/crypt/tunnel minimap images from the same client, with soft transparent edges. This artwork remains the property of its respective owners.

`Media/GatherVisuals33` contains code-generated geometric rings and circular progress swipes. These assets contain no copied game artwork.

`Media/Transports14` contains independently drawn geometric boat and zeppelin glyphs. Native route/terminal coordinates are facts extracted from this installation's TaxiPathNode table. Seven timing seed sets were inspected in OctoBus; no OctoBus addon code or artwork is included. See SOURCE.md for attribution and timing limits.
